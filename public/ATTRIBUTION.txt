Atelier Atlas — demonstration collection

Artist names were supplied for this demo. Locations, biographies and sample artwork titles are illustrative and do not assert facts about real people.

The eight PNG paintings named for artist slugs in /art are AI-generated sample artworks created with OpenAI image generation for Atelier Atlas. They are not works supplied by those artists.
