# Atelier Atlas — narrated demo

## Art deserves more than a feed.

What if discovering art felt less like scrolling, and more like stepping into another world?

## 01 / Discover a place

Meet Atelier Atlas. A world of independent art, where every golden pin is an invitation to discover a different perspective.

## One city. Multiple perspectives.

Choose a place on the globe. Even artists who share a city have their own space to tell their story.

## 02 / Step inside the exhibition

Enter an artist's three dimensional gallery. Follow a guided view, move between artworks, and open a painting to discover its title, materials, and meaning.

## Explore at your own pace

Or take your own path. Walk through the room, drag to look around, and expand the exhibition. Gallery layouts adapt to the collection and each painting's proportions.

## An artist-selected soundtrack

Artists can connect a Spotify playlist, adding another layer to their exhibition. Playback stays in Spotify's own controls.

## More ways to experience art

A complete two dimensional collection offers another way to explore, alongside keyboard access, mobile controls, and reduced motion support.

## 03 / Make a place for your art

And you can become part of the atlas. Create a profile, place your pin, upload your own artwork, and give it a title and description. Connect a playlist, then save your gallery. In this prototype, contributions stay in your browser.

## Your pin. Your gallery. Your story.

Your new pin opens a gallery built around your work. Next, we want to make these exhibitions persistent and shared.

## Atelier Atlas

Atelier Atlas. A world of art, a space of your own. Discover it at atelier atlas dot vercel dot app.
